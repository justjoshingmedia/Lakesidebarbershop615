Our Story

Where the Lake Meets the Chair

Nestled along the shores of Nashville, Lakeside Barbershop 615 brings together the timeless craft of traditional barbering and the relaxed, easy spirit of lakeside living. We believe every cut should feel like a breath of fresh air.

From hot towel shaves to precision fades, our barbers are craftsmen first — taking the time to listen, consult, and deliver a look as sharp as the Nashville skyline reflecting off the water.
